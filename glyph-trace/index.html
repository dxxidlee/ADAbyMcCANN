This tool traces a word or single-letter image into bezier outlines and exports them as an OTF typeface.

Guide

Type the word, mind the case, then split and trace. Non-touching letters split on their own; drag the dashed crop edges to separate letters that touch. The black outline is the fitted bezier — click a tile to edit its points.

Editing

Drag the squares to move points, the small circles to bend the curve. Drag a box over several points to select them, or shift-click, then move or delete them together. Points snap to align with others and a red guide shows the match. Double-click the outline to add a point. Square node is a corner, round is smooth.
